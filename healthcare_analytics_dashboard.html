Statistical Box Plot & Outlier Point Cloud Upper Fence = Q3 + 1.5 × IQR

Statistical Methodology

In healthcare econometric analysis, doctor visits are inherently zero-inflated; more than 75% of individuals typically seek 0 visits in a two-week retrospective window, rendering Q1 and often Q3 zero.

Under standard Tukey methodology: Upper Fence = Q3 + 1.5 × (Q3 - Q1)

Patients who visit a physician multiple times within a short two-week span represent high-utilization cohorts requiring dedicated clinical case management.

Dataset Variable Dictionary & Definitions

visits
Number of doctor visits recorded in the past 2 weeks (Dependent variable).
gender
Biological sex of respondent (Categorical: female or male).
age
Age in years divided by 100 (e.g. 0.19 = 19 yrs, 0.72 = 72 yrs) as coded in Cameron & Trivedi (1986).
income
Annual household income in tens of thousands of Australian dollars (normalized scale).
illness
Number of self-reported illnesses or health conditions experienced during the past 2 weeks (0 to 5+).
reduced
Number of days of reduced activity / incapacity due to illness in the past 2 weeks (0 to 14 days).
health
General health questionnaire score based on Goldberg's General Health scale (higher score indicates poorer health status).
private
Private health insurance coverage status (yes or no).
freepoor
Access to free healthcare through government program for low-income persons (yes or no).
freerepat
Free healthcare coverage through veterans / repatriation entitlements (yes or no).
nchronic
Number of non-limiting chronic health conditions (0, 1, or 2+).
lchronic
Long-term limiting chronic condition indicator (yes: condition limits activity, no).
